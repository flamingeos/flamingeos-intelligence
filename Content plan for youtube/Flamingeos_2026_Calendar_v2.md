## 🎯 The Formula

Every video falls into one of these FORMATS — inspired by the channels you love:

| Format | Inspired By | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **PRANK + SURPRISE** | ThatWasEpic, Lofe | Destroy something → upgrade it, public embarrassment pranks |
| **MINI CHALLENGE** | Michelle Khare, Brian Carmichael | "I trained like X," "$0 for 24hrs," time-limited tests |

## 📸 THUMBNAIL CHEAT SHEET

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Your face** | BIG. Always showing emotion — shock, pain, fear, joy, disbelief |
| **Text** | 2-4 words MAX. Bold. White or yellow on dark. Or black on bright |
| **Context** | One clear visual that explains the situation instantly |
| **Colors** | Orange, red, yellow, neon green pop hardest in the feed |
| **Contrast** | Bright subject, blurred or dark background |
| **Split** | Before/after or two-panel works for challenge/transformation videos |
| **NO clutter** | If you can't understand the thumbnail in 1 second, simplify it |

## ⚡ SHORTS EXTRACTION GUIDE

Every long-form video should give you 2-4 Shorts. Here's what to clip:

| Video Type | Best Short Clip |
|---|---|
| Prank | Single best reaction (the scream, the laugh, the anger) |
| Challenge | The hardest moment / almost-quit moment |
| Absurd | The funniest failure |
| Adrenaline | The biggest jump/stunt in slow-mo |
| Giving | The most emotional reaction |
| Investigative | The most shocking discovery |

**Post schedule:**
- Short 1: Same day as main video (Saturday)
- Short 2: Monday
- Short 3: Wednesday
